INT. LATE-NIGHT NEWSROOM -- 1969

MAYA hunches over a wire-service printer as it chatters out fresh copy.

MAYA
Apollo 11 landed on the Moon on July 20th, 1969.
Neil Armstrong was the first person to walk on it.

DEREK
(not looking up)
The Berlin Wall came down that same summer, right?

MAYA
No -- that's 1989. You're twenty years off.

DEREK
Fair. Did you know Marie Curie won two Nobel Prizes?

MAYA
Physics and Chemistry. First person ever to do that.
